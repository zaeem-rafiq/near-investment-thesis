NEAR through 2031: Not yet

Not yet: public data through November 1, 2026 does not support opening or adding to a NEAR position for a hold through 2031, because only one of three locked tests passed and three accounts made up 96.6% of that pass.

For new money: this evidence does not support buying NEAR now for a hold through 2031.

For current holders: this evidence does not support adding, and by itself it does not say to sell.

General research opinion on public data, not personal advice. No portfolio share is given.

Claim

Check 1's pass does not show broad demand: without three accounts, net buying falls from 77.75% to 2.66% of issuance value.

Evidence for

Burn plus buybacks covered 15.08% of issuance; signers outside the frozen biggest receivers grew 14.58%. Both failed their 20% bars. Overall daily signers fell 73.1%.

Evidence against

Check 1 passed at 77.75% of issuance value. 1Click volume grew +362.9%; that does not establish token demand. Conditional HSP-029 adoption would lower the buying needed to offset supply.

What would change my mind

Only the locked review rules decide the call: zero or one pass gives Not yet; two or three gives Building. Own requires all three in a non-overlapping window, first eligible 2027-11-01. Broken overrides these calls under the unchanged downgrade rules. Missing data counts as not passing.

If HSP-029 is vetoed or not adopted, the call stays Not yet and the 2031 buying need moves from the HSP-029 rows to the 2.5% cap rows of the existing table. Veto status alone does not rerun the checks.
