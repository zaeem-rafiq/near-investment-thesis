NEAR through 2031: Not yet

Not yet: public data through November 1, 2026 does not support opening or adding to a NEAR position for a hold through 2031, because only one of three locked tests passed and three accounts made up 96.6% of that pass.

For new money: this evidence does not support buying NEAR now for a hold through 2031.

For current holders: this evidence does not support adding, and by itself it does not say to sell.

General research opinion on public data, not personal advice. No portfolio share is given.

Claim

Public data through November 1, 2026 does not yet support a NEAR hold through 2031. The one passing test does not show broad demand: without three accounts, net buying falls from 77.75% to 2.66% of the value of new NEAR.

Evidence for

  • Burn plus buybacks offset 15.08% of new NEAR, short of the 20% bar. About 98% of buyback receipts had not left the three wallets by November 1, and held NEAR is not burned.
  • Signers outside the ten biggest apps grew 14.58%, short of the 20% bar, while all daily signers fell 73.1%.
  • NEAR was on one side of only 2.07% of 1Click swap volume.

Evidence against

  • 1Click users bought $52.08M more NEAR than they sold, 77.75% of the value of new NEAR. Removing accounts that both bought and sold still leaves 44.74% to 50.47%.
  • Buyback receipts of 3.13M NEAR in 36 weeks match DefiLlama within 0.04%.
  • The usage drop is concentrated: the ten biggest apps explain all of it, and everyone else grew.
  • If adopted, HSP-029 lowers the yearly buying needed in 2031 from $154M to $94.8M at the latest close.

What would change my mind

  • Up: two passing checks at a review give Building. The two closest are burn plus buybacks, 15.08% now against 20%, and usage outside the ten biggest apps, +14.58% now against +20%.
  • Down: the thesis is Broken if Check 1 and Check 2 both fall below 10% at two reviews in a row, or if the revenue wallets receive no NEAR from outside for 13 weeks.

If HSP-029 is vetoed or not adopted, the call stays Not yet and the 2031 buying need moves from the HSP-029 rows to the 2.5% cap rows of the existing table. Veto status alone does not rerun the checks.
