NEAR through 2031: Not yet

First we tested T1–T3; none passed. Then we wrote three position checks and their 20% bars before computing them, and time-stamped the rules. One passed.

Rating: hold, do not buy, through 2031. "Not yet" is the rule's label for this call, as addenda 2 and 3 define it.

  • New money: does not support buying now.
  • Staked holder: does not say to sell; yields 4.97% and 5.52% (two estimates, because the on-chain protocol_reward_rate and the HSP-029 post disagree on the treasury share), before validator commission and tax, are above gross issuance 2.51%.
  • Unstaked holder: does not say to sell, but unstaked holding lost 2.41% of its supply share over the year.

Holder facts

Control: the bought NEAR is held by two Sputnik DAOs and one multisig. Spending needs two approvals in each DAO and two confirmations in the multisig; none of the three has a full-access key; one DAO's admin role can change its rules with a single vote. DefiLlama's holders revenue matches the wallet receipts within 0.04%. That is a consistency check, not independent confirmation, because DefiLlama measures revenue from the same wallets.

Claim

Demand is concentrated: without three accounts, net buying falls from 77.75% to 2.66% of the value of new NEAR.

All three are 64-hex implicit accounts. The largest bought $27.2M in 374 swaps over 3 weeks, paid 100% in ZEC, 90% of it from May 22 to 27, 2026, sold $0 through 1Click and holds no native NEAR; the other two bought $16.9M over 26 weeks and $8.26M over 7, sold $1.98M and $77.3K, and hold 3.05 and 3.05 NEAR (public RPC, 2026-11-10). The data cannot tell whether they are exchanges, market makers or long-term buyers.

Why: evidence for Not yet

  • Check 2: about 98% of receipts remained in the wallets by November 1; held is not burned.
  • Check 3: everyone else grew, but all daily signers fell 73.1%.
  • Intents is chain-agnostic by design. NEAR was on one side of only 2.07% of 1Click volume, so volume growth does not translate directly into NEAR demand.

Author's view: the author weighs Check 2 most, because burn and buybacks are the only channel where NEAR Intents revenue reaches holders, while Check 1's pass rests on three accounts.

The case for NEAR

  • Net buying was $52.08M, 77.75% of issuance value; latest 13-week 1Click volume was $3.8B, up +362.9%.
  • The ten biggest apps explain the usage decline; everyone else grew. Holders-revenue wallet receipts were $7.5M in 36 weeks.

Limit: Check 1 counts 1Click swaps only, not trading on exchanges or other venues, so it does not measure total demand for NEAR.
