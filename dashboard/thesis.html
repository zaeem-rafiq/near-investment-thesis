NEAR through 2031: Not yet

First we tested T1–T3; none passed. Then we wrote three position checks and their 20% bars before computing them, and time-stamped the rules. One passed. The evidence records the intervening provisional results and addenda.

Call and position

Not yet: public data through November 1, 2026 does not support opening or adding to a NEAR position for a hold through 2031, because only one of three locked tests passed and three accounts made up 96.6% of that pass.

For new money: this evidence does not support buying NEAR now for a hold through 2031.

For current holders: this evidence does not support adding, and by itself it does not say to sell.

In rating terms: hold, do not buy.

Size band, as a share of the reader's own full position: none now, at most a quarter at Building, full at Own.

Holder facts

An unstaked holder's supply share fell 2.41%. Gross staking-yield estimates of 4.97% and 5.52% exceed gross issuance of 2.51%, so staking offsets dilution before fees and tax.

Claim

Demand is concentrated: without three accounts, net buying falls from 77.75% to 2.66% of the value of new NEAR.

Why: evidence for Not yet

  • Burn plus buybacks offset 15.08% of new NEAR, below 20%. About 98% of receipts remained in the wallets by November 1; held is not burned.
  • Signers outside the ten biggest apps, fixed by the earlier year's ranking, grew 14.58%, below 20%; all daily signers fell 73.1%.
  • Intents is chain-agnostic by design. NEAR was on one side of only 2.07% of 1Click volume, so volume growth does not translate directly into NEAR demand.

The case for NEAR

  • Net buying was $52.08M, 77.75% of issuance value.
  • Latest 13-week 1Click volume was $3.8B, up +362.9%.
  • Holders-revenue wallet receipts were $7.5M in 36 weeks, matching DefiLlama within 0.04%.
  • The ten biggest apps explain the usage decline; everyone else grew.
