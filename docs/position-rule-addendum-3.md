# Position rule, addendum 3

Written on 2026-11-09, after Checks 1 to 3, every cut and the first call, Not yet, were computed. Before writing it, the author had seen DefiLlama's list of chain revenue and the market caps of six chains during a feasibility check. No peer table had been computed. It changes nothing in docs/position-rule.md or addendum 2: no check, threshold, result or call changes.

Size band. Each call carries a size band, stated as a share of the reader's own full position, meaning the most the reader would hold in NEAR under this thesis. It is never a share of a portfolio. It is general research language, not personal advice, and the fractions are the author's choice, not derived from data. Not yet: none, so no new money and no adding. Building: at most a quarter of a full position. Own: a full position. Broken: none, and the case for holding NEAR on this thesis is withdrawn.

Peer comparison. One table, labeled not official, compares NEAR with two other chains on market cap and yearly revenue. Every figure comes from DefiLlama's public API, pulled once and pinned with the request URLs and the retrieval time. Yearly revenue is the sum of the chain's daily revenue in DefiLlama's Chain category from 2025-11-03 to 2026-11-01. Market cap is DefiLlama's coins value for the chain's native token at the pull time. Candidates are Chain-category entries whose DefiLlama chain record has a gecko_id equal to its gasTokenGeckoId, which leaves out chains that pay gas in another chain's token. Rank candidates by market cap and pick the nearest one above NEAR and the nearest one below. If a pick has no complete revenue series for the window, take the next one on the same side. NEAR's row uses the same definitions, which differ from this thesis's own burn and buyback measures. A second NEAR row adds DefiLlama's holders revenue for NEAR Intents over the same window, labeled as such.
