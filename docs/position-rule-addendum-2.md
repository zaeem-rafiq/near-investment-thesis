# Position rule, addendum 2

Written on 2026-11-09, after the first Check 1 total, its breakdowns, press and DefiLlama figures on buybacks and the revenue wallets' current balances were known, and before Check 1 final, Check 2, Check 3 or any cut in addendum 1 was computed. It changes nothing in docs/position-rule.md. T1, T2 and T3, Checks 1 to 3, their 20% bars and the first call stay as written.

What each call means. These are general research opinions on public data, not personal advice, and none gives a portfolio share. The horizon is the end of 2031. Not yet: neutral. This evidence does not support opening a position in NEAR or adding to one, and it does not by itself say to sell. Building: constructive. This evidence supports a small position at most, and does not support a full one. Own: positive. This evidence supports holding a full position through the horizon. Broken: negative. This evidence withdraws the case for holding NEAR on this thesis.

Review dates. On or after 2026-11-16, record whether HSP-029 was vetoed. No check runs on that date. Recompute Check 1 final, Check 2 and Check 3 at four re-runs, each over the 52 weeks that end on the Sunday before its date: 2027-02-01, 2027-05-03, 2027-08-02 and 2027-11-01. Check 2 uses only weeks from 2026-02-23 onward. Check 3 compares the last 52 weeks with the 52 weeks before them and keeps the first run's frozen top 10.

Own. A later 52-week window means one that does not overlap 2025-11-03 to 2026-11-01. The first is the re-run dated 2027-11-01, so no earlier re-run can give Own.

Downgrade. Each re-run sets the call again from that re-run's checks. Zero or one check passing gives Not yet, whatever the call was before. Two or three give Building. Own needs all three in a non-overlapping window.

Thesis broken. The thesis is broken if Check 1 final and Check 2 are both below 10%, half of their 20% bars, at two re-runs in a row. It is also broken at any re-run where the three revenue wallets received no NEAR or wNEAR from outside in the last 13 weeks of the window. Broken replaces the call and stays until a new rule is written and time-stamped.

Missing data. A source that cannot be measured at a re-run is reported as not measurable, and its check counts as not passing.
