# Brand brief

## Product

A public research dashboard and one-page thesis that tests the long-term case for holding NEAR against public on-chain data. The three position checks and their pass bars were written before they were computed, and the rule was time-stamped. The README states the exact order of rules and results. Every number reruns offline from committed data.

- **Audience:** crypto investors and fund analysts who distrust hype, and hackathon judges (human and AI) who check the work.
- **Brand sentence:** "We wrote the pass bars before we computed the checks, and every number reruns on your laptop."
- **Personality:** exact, candid, calm. **Must not feel:** hyped, salesy, smug.
- **Where it lives:** the dashboard (web, 375 to 1440 px), the README on GitHub, a favicon, a social card for the X demo post. No phone app.

## Constraints and assumptions

- The product has no name. It is "NEAR / Evidence ledger" in the nav today. Each direction proposes its own working name, because the name carries the strategy.
- "NEAR" is the NEAR Foundation's mark. The identity must not use NEAR's logo, its "N" shape or its colors, so the dashboard never looks official. "NEAR" stays only in descriptive text such as "NEAR through 2031".
- Status colors must stay readable: pass and fail are the core of the first screen. Red must mean fail only.
- The current look is neutral grey (Inter, #171717 accent). Nothing is kept by request.
- Deadline: the hackathon closes 2026-11-14 23:59 UTC. The owner chose the full exploration with this timing known.

## Category study

Done from known products and from other public NEAR thesis dashboards. The sites were not revisited on 2026-11-14.

| Product | What it does well |
|---|---|
| Messari | Dense research pages with a clear source line on each figure |
| Token Terminal | Treats chains like listed companies: revenue, earnings, one table |
| Glassnode | Charts first, each with a named metric and a definition |
| Dune | Every chart links to the query that made it |
| Strong NEAR thesis dashboards | One big number or verdict on the first screen |

Outside the category, with the same personality:

| Product | What it does well |
|---|---|
| Consumer Reports | A rating stated plainly, then the test method behind it |
| Our World in Data | Every chart carries its source and can be downloaded |

**Category clichés to avoid:** coins, rockets and moons, candlestick charts, glowing neon on black, purple-to-blue gradients, hexagons, chain links and blocks, laser eyes, gold on navy. Also NEAR's own black-and-white "N".

**What the study found:** crypto research brands look like trading terminals (dark, dense, neon). Nobody in the category looks like a notary, a lab notebook or a tabloid. Each direction takes one of those.
